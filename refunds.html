Last updated: November 2026

Order confirmation

Gift orders are subject to product availability, customization review and delivery-area confirmation. An event enquiry is not a confirmed booking until the services, price and cancellation terms are accepted by both parties.

Gift cancellations

For a non-customized gift, request cancellation within seven calendar days of placing the order. If accepted, the cancellation is free and the refund will be initiated within seven calendar days after approval, using the original payment method where possible. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to post it. A customized gift cannot be cancelled or returned for a change of mind once the order is placed. This does not limit applicable rights for an item that is defective, damaged, incorrect or not as described; contact us promptly so we can review and resolve the issue.

Event bookings

An event enquiry is not a booking until the details and terms are confirmed. Once an event is booked, customer cancellation is not available as a free cancellation; any cancellation request will be subject to the cancellation charge and terms disclosed and agreed before booking. If surprise4u cancels, any applicable charge will be no less than the charge applied to the customer in equivalent circumstances.

Refunds and order issues

For an approved refund, we will initiate payment within seven calendar days of approval through the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider posting times can vary. For damaged, defective, incorrect or undelivered items, contact us with your order reference and details so we can review the issue. Applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.
