Information collected

When you submit a gift order or event enquiry, the site may collect names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, event details, personalization text, selected products and order information.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, coordinate customization and delivery, manage orders and provide customer support. Payment-card details are entered in the payment provider's hosted checkout and are not requested in this website form.

AI chat

If you use the website chat assistant, the messages you submit and recent chat context are sent to OpenAI's API to generate a reply. Do not include payment details or other sensitive personal information in chat. The assistant cannot access order records or complete purchases, bookings, cancellations, or refunds.

Storage and access

This prototype stores submitted order information on its application server. Before public launch, the operator must configure secure production hosting, access controls, backups and a retention schedule. Do not submit real customer data while this prototype is running locally or on temporary hosting.

Sharing

Information should be shared only with service providers needed to process a requested order, such as delivery or event vendors, and the payment provider. The business must specify the actual providers and applicable sharing practices before launch.

Retention and contact

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Privacy contact: surprise.us.with.mail@gmail.com or +91 77828 26601. Order records are retained only as needed to handle the order, support, accounting and legal obligations; set and follow a documented retention schedule before launch.
